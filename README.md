This is a management-lending system for General Service Office for university.
General Service Office is the office in a university that lends equipment to the school's staff, faculty, as well as students. 
This system is develop to aid assistance for them in managing their equipments and monitoring lending.
It as automated emailing for when the equipment's is at critical quantity level.
Emails the borrowers when their request to borrow is approve or denied.
Has a borrower interface.
